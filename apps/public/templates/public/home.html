{% translate "It tells you how to tell." %}

{% blocktranslate %}Nobody is born knowing the difference between medium and high acidity. So every answer carries the technique: swallow, close your mouth, and count how long you keep watering. Tannin is a dry drag on the gums, not a taste. Body is the milk test — skimmed, semi-skimmed or double cream.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "You describe. It deduces." %}

{% blocktranslate %}You are never asked whether the wine went through malolactic conversion — that is the thing you came to learn. You record what you can actually smell, and the app names the process: brioche means time on the lees, butter means malolactic, banana means carbonic maceration. It shows its working, so next time you know.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %}Every tap is saved on your phone before it is sent anywhere, so a cellar with no signal changes nothing. Finish a tasting whenever you like — a note that stops at Smell is still a note worth keeping.{% endblocktranslate %}
